🩺 Diagnostic Thresholds & Clinical Interpretation Guide
Pressure-Volume Index (PVI)
• Normal Adult: 18 – 30 mL (mean ~25 mL)
• Borderline / Elderly: 13 – 18 mL
• Severely Reduced (< 13 mL): Indicates severe cranial volume exhaustion or high elastance.
Resistance to Outflow (Rout)
• Normal: < 10 mmHg/(mL/min)
• Borderline: 10 – 12 mmHg/(mL/min)
• Pathological (> 12 mmHg/(mL/min)): High probability of clinical improvement following CSF diversion / shunt surgery in suspected iNPH.
ICP Safety Protocol
• Default Safety Limit: 30 mmHg (represented by the red dashed line).
• If induced peak pressure exceeds the safety limit, the calculator flags a high-priority alert. Bolus injection should be immediately halted if clinical threshold is reached.

📖 Marmarou Bolus Model Mathematical Framework

The Marmarou Bolus Injection/Withdrawal Method determines intracranial mechanical compliance and CSF absorption resistance through a rapid volume pertubation (±ΔV) introduced over a few seconds into the lumbar or ventricular CSF space.

1. Pressure-Volume Index (PVI)
PVI = ΔV / log₁₀(Pₚ / P₀) [mL]
PVI represents the theoretical volume increment required to raise intracranial pressure by one order of magnitude (10-fold).
2. Cranial Elastance & Compliance at Baseline
E₀ = (ln(10) × P₀) / PVI = (2.3026 × P₀) / PVI [mmHg/mL]
C₀ = 1 / E₀ = PVI / (2.3026 × P₀) [mL/mmHg]
3. CSF Outflow Resistance (Rout)
Rout = [t × P₀] / [PVI × log₁₀( (Pt(Pₚ - P₀)) / (Pₚ(Pt - P₀)) )]
Where t is elapsed time post-bolus, and Pt is the ICP measured at time t during pressure decay.
4. Pressure Decay Curve P(t) & Derivatives
P(t) = P₀ / [ 1 - (1 - P₀/Pₚ) × exp(-t / τ) ]
where τ = (Rout × PVI) / (2.3026 × P₀)
The first derivative dP/dt reflects the instant rate of CSF absorption, decelerating to 0 as pressure restores baseline P₀.
